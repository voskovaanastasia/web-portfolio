# Default OG/Twitter share image — design spec

Replaces `public/og-image.png`, currently a placeholder generated by
`scripts/generate-og-images.cjs`. This is the card shown when `/`, `/about`,
`/projects`, and any case-study route without its own image (currently only
`/project/tax-compliance`) are shared on LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Slack, etc.

## Dimensions

- **1200 × 630 px**, exactly. This is the standard `og:image` size (1.91:1)
  that Facebook, LinkedIn, Slack and Twitter's `summary_large_image` card all
  expect without cropping or letterboxing.
- Export as PNG or JPEG (not WebP — some crawlers, notably older Facebook
  scrapers, still don't reliably fetch WebP for link previews).

## Safe area

Some clients crop the edges (LinkedIn's mobile feed in particular sometimes
shows a tighter, more square-ish crop). Keep all essential content — logo,
name, title — inside a **centered 1000 × 525 px safe area** (100px margin
left/right, ~52px top/bottom). Background color/texture can bleed to the
full 1200 × 630 canvas.

## Minimum legible text size

LinkedIn renders the desktop feed card at roughly **520px wide** — about a
0.43× scale-down from the 1200px source. Anything smaller than the numbers
below will blur or vanish at that size:

- **Headline (name/title)**: 60px minimum at 1200px canvas width (renders ≈26px on LinkedIn's card).
- **Secondary line (role, tagline)**: 36px minimum (renders ≈15px).
- Avoid a third tier of text smaller than that — it won't survive the downscale on any platform.

## File-size budget

- **Target ≤ 300KB.** Crawlers time out on slow fetches and some re-fetch on
  every share, so smaller is better.
- **Hard cap 1MB** — safely under every platform's actual limit (Facebook 8MB,
  LinkedIn 5MB) but keeps load fast everywhere.

## Brand assets to use

- **AV logo mark** — `src/assets/logo-av.svg` (same mark as the favicon and
  nav logo). Use it large and singular, not the full "ANASTASIIA VOSKOVA"
  wordmark — it needs to read at thumbnail size.
- **`#288fd6`** (brand blue) as the dominant background or a strong accent —
  it's the site's one consistent color across every section.
- **Space Grotesk** (`font-grotesk`, weight 500/700) for the name/headline.
- **Courier New** (`font-mono-bold`) for a short kicker/label line, the same
  monospace accent used site-wide for section labels like "How I work" and
  "PROBLEM" — e.g. a small "PRODUCT DESIGNER" tag above or below the name.

## Content

Keep it to three elements, per the safe-area/legibility limits above:
1. AV mark
2. "Anastasiia Voskova" (headline)
3. "Product Designer" or a short one-line value prop (secondary line)

Nothing else — no screenshots, no case-study imagery (those already get
their own dedicated OG image per route).
